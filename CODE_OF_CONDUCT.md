# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing the observable technical issue.
- Welcome questions and make room for contributors with different experience.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's data or credentials.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and correct mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, sustained personal
attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing, or publishing private information are
not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Report
conduct concerns through the repository's private maintainer contact channel.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers involved
in a report must recuse themselves from its resolution.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone publicly represents
the project.
